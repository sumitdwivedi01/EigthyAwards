One DNS record
  • Best for the brand: awards.face-cii.in. Their main website stays untouched; the award lives on their own domain.
  • Connecting takes one DNS record added by their IT person; HTTPS (the padlock) is created automatically. Try it on page 3.

Honest limits

  • A structured builder, not a free-design tool like Wix: no dragging anywhere, no custom code. That's what keeps 80 sites safe and good-looking.
  • Fonts come from a curated list (licensing and speed).
  • An organiser who wants a fully custom design can keep their own website and link its "Apply" button to our branded form.
  • Level 3 domains need the organiser to add one DNS record; we check our hosting plan's domain limit before promising many.
