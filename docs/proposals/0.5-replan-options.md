# Re-plan options: a 15 October demo, or the real product

> **Status: closed (9 Oct 2026).** The lead chose a third way: **three phases**, with Phase 1 a fully working platform by 15 Oct (close to Option A, and deployed online), then Phases 2 and 3 (close to Option B). See [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md). Kept for history.
> 8 Oct 2026 · Related: [UI overview](../ui/README.md) · [the full plan](../PHASES.md) · [new issues and decisions](0.4-new-issues.md)

## Why re-plan

- The scope grew a lot through the client's answers: on-site rounds, the Leader's PA, data consistency, **branded award sites with a page builder**, proof documents, entry limits.
- The plan in PHASES.md has **15 build phases** and ran to about Day 21. The owner can go **no later than 15 October**: about **5 working days** from the 9 Oct call (Fri 9, Mon 12 to Thu 15), or 7 if the weekend is worked.
- The owner's view: building a big demo in a few days isn't efficient. It wouldn't become the final product, and a real product of this depth needs much more time.

So there are two honest choices, below. They are not exclusive: **A can be the first step of B**.

---

## Option A: a focused demo by 15 October

**Goal:** exactly what the brief asks, and done properly. "Build something small that runs. It should show one system handling **two awards that work in different ways**. A staff member should be able to set those differences **without changing any code**." Plus the **four rules**, enforced and tested.

**Built and running** (on the real architecture, so none of it is thrown away):

| Area | In the demo |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Phase 1 as built (62 tests), plus the tables for sites, proof documents and the entry limit |
| People | Email and password login; roles and organisations seeded; PAN / GSTIN checks |
| Award setup on screen | Dates, fee, blind judging, entry limit, categories; a simple questionnaire editor with published versions (**R4**); a scoring sheet with weight checks; one written round |
| Applying | Dynamic form with autosave; proof documents; submit with the entry limit ("499 / 500"); deadline lock |
| Judging | Proof check; masking of answers (**R1**); recorded conflicts and assignment (**R2**); scoring; score change with a reason (**R3**) |
| Results | The department head approves; staff publish Shortlisted / Rejected |
| Branding | A public **branded award site** for each award, rendered from sections (about 6 section types), edited with a simple form-based editor |
| Proof | Two seeded awards with clearly different settings; tests for R1–R4; a README to run it locally |

**Shown with the prototype only** (designed and documented, not built): on-site rounds and medals; the full site builder (layouts, versions screen, restore); own domains; Leader / PA admin screens and dashboards; master data screens; emails beyond the log; deployment (it runs locally).

**Day plan (draft):**

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Fri 9 Oct | Lead call. Merge Phase 1 (+ new tables). Seed both awards |
| Mon 12 Oct | Login, roles, organisations; award setup screens (settings, categories) |

2. the masking of files (keep answers);
3. emails.

The four rules and the two contrasting awards are never cut.

**Effort:** about 5 working days (7 with the weekend).

## Option B: the real product, done properly

**Goal:** a product the client can actually run for 80 awards: everything in the spec, at production quality.

**Scope:** all 15 build phases in [PHASES.md](../PHASES.md), plus what a real launch needs and a demo doesn't:
- security review and penetration test;
- an accessibility check;
- load testing near deadlines;
- a data-protection (DPDP Act) review of the proof documents;
- a production email domain;
- backups and monitoring;
- the client's acceptance testing (UAT);
- a real payment gateway, if fees are charged.

**Estimate** (one developer with AI help; about 1.5–2 days per phase at production quality, not 1):

| Milestone | Phases | Working days |
|---|---|---|
| M1 · Foundation and people | 1–2 | 6–8 |
| M2 · Award setup, branded sites and the builder | 3–6 | 12–15 |
| M3 · Applications, proof and judging (the four rules) | 7–10 | 12–15 |
| M4 · Results, on-site rounds and dashboards | 11–13 | 8–10 |
| M5 · Production readiness and deployment | 14–15, plus the list above | 8–10 |
| Client UAT and fixes | — | 5–10 |
| **Total** | | **about 50–70 working days (10–14 weeks)** for one developer; about **6–8 weeks** with two |

Each milestone ends with something the client can see and test, and the plan, progress log and decision records continue exactly as now.

---

## Recommendation

**Do A by 15 October, and present B as the roadmap.**

- A is what the brief literally asks for, and it proves the hard parts: one engine, two different awards, no code per award, and the four rules with tests.
- A is built on the real architecture, Phase 1 foundation included, so **it's the first slice of B, not a throw-away demo**. That answers the concern that a big demo wouldn't become the final product: A stays small, and B grows from it.
- The prototype ([docs/ui](../ui/README.md)) and the branding presentation show the client the **whole** product today, including what A doesn't build yet.
