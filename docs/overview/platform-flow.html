1 · The problem

Eighty awards, eighty separate ways of working

An Indian industry body runs about 80 awards, from business excellence to energy, safety and shop-floor Kaizen. Each award grew up on its own system.

Award website"Acme Steel Ltd"
Member portal"ACME STEEL LIMITED"
Largest award's portal"Acme Steels, Pune unit"
→
One company record
Acme Steel Ltd · one PAN
Used by every award, never copied
🧭

A different system each time

Applicants, jury and staff learn a new tool for every award.

🧩

Data nobody trusts

The same company is written many ways, so there is no true combined view.

🛠️

A developer for every change

Staff can't start a new award or change its questions on their own.

2 · The idea

One shared journey, different settings

An award is settings, not code. Staff choose the settings on screen, so a new award needs no developer.
Built once · shared by all 80
  • Logins and roles
  • One record per company and person
  • Applying, deadlines, locking
  • Judging, approval, results, emails
  • A history of every important change
  • Proof checks and entry limits
  • The site builder
Chosen per award · by staff
  • Questions and sections
  • Scoring sheet and weights
  • Dates and entry fee
  • Blind judging on or off
  • Categories and rounds
  • The award's own branded site

6 · The four rules

Promises the platform always keeps

1

Blind judging

In a blind award, jury only see a cleaned copy, never the company's name.

2

No conflicts

Record "this juror knows this company" once, and that pairing is refused everywhere.

3

Score history

Every change after submission keeps who, when, old, new and why. Approved scores never change.

4

Old forms still open

Last year's applications always open with last year's questions.

7 · Why this way

What happens when award number 81 starts?

We compared three ways to build it. The red steps are where a developer or a second copy of the data comes in.

A · Site per awardToday's approach
Staff ask→Developer copies a site→Company typed in again→Live after weeks
B · Fixed award typesFast until an award doesn't fit
Staff pick a type→Fits?→ no →Developer adds a type→Live
C · Configurable ✔Our choice
Staff fill settings→System checks them→Live the same day
